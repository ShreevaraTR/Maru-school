WORD 01
인사
안an녕nyeong하ha세se요yo

annyeonghaseyo

Hello (polite)

Your go-to greeting for almost anyone: shop staff, teachers, new people you meet.

마루MARU
03 / 07
WORD 02
감사
감gam사sa합ham니ni다da

gamsahamnida

Thank you (formal)

It’s spelled 합 but said “ham”: ㅂ turns into ㅁ before ㄴ. Korean has sound rules like this, and they’re learnable.

마루MARU
04 / 07
WORD 03
예
네

ne

Yes

You’ll also hear 네 as a friendly “uh-huh, I’m listening” in conversation.

마루MARU
05 / 07
WORD 04
부정
아a니ni요yo

aniyo

No (polite)

That final 요 (yo) is what makes it polite. Spot it on 안녕하세요 too?

마루MARU
06 / 07
WORD 05
부탁
주ju세se요yo

juseyo

Please give me… / …, please

Put it after what you want: 물 주세요 (mul juseyo) = “Water, please.”
